## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the ethos of strategic B2B support, rigorous corporate discipline, and contemporary executive elegance. It projects high-tier competence, discretion, and flawless organizational structure for AMANI SERVICES across administrative execution, executive secretarial operations, and commercial strategy.

The aesthetic direction merges **Executive Minimalism** with **Modern Architectural Precision**:
- Pristine, breathing whitespace balanced against deliberate structural frameworks.
- Subtle luxury articulated via muted metallic gold accents against a spectrum of deep architectural navies.
- Controlled information density that handles dense corporate governance, operational workflows, and data tracking with zero visual clutter.
- Tactile reassurance driven by hairline structural boundaries, crisp status cues, and quiet state transitions.

## Colors
The palette leverages a classical institutional foundation elevated by modern SaaS contrast standards and quiet luxury accents.

- **Primary (`#142B45`)**: Deep Navy. Serves as the primary anchor for dominant structural elements, primary headers, key interactive triggers, and high-impact navigational headers.
- **Secondary (`#254E70`)**: Institutional Slate Blue. Used for sub-navigation, secondary interactive elements, selected container states, and supporting accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A46D`)**: Discreet Warm Gold. Applied with strict restraint for VIP/Premium indicators, high-priority strategic advisory tags, critical highlight anchors, and micro-accents.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray. Deployed for body copy, contextual descriptions, subtle dividers, and non-active icons.
- **Canvas & Containers**: Layered between pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and forms, and stratified foundational grays (`#F8FAFC`, `#F5F7FA`) for canvas canvas backdrop and table track backgrounds.
- **System States**: Success is strictly anchored by `#16856A` (Emerald Pine) for valid compliance verifications, successful filings, and positive deliverables.

## Typography
The system employs an authoritative dual-engine typographic hierarchy:

1. **Public Showcase & High-Level Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and structural prestige to all displays, hero statements, and category headings. Its tight proportions and modern terminals evoke institutional confidence without visual stiffness.
2. **Operations, Data & Functional Core (`Inter`)**: Drives all body text, dashboard modules, data dense matrices, and operational labels. Chosen for its neutral, highly engineered legibility across tabular numbers and compact data cells.

### Formatting Rules
- Display titles must maintain tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`) to retain visual density.
- Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced globally on data tables, financial figures, dates, and administrative identifiers.
- Metadata and uppercase badge indicators (`label-sm`) require positive letter spacing (`0.03em` to `0.05em`) for immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 12-column grid engine tuned for executive clarity and enterprise administration.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max canvas container of 1440px. Gutters set to `gutter-desktop` (32px), outer margin at `margin-desktop` (48px). SaaS modules collapse secondary navigation into an 80px / 260px expandable dock.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid with `gutter` (24px) and `margin-tablet` (32px). Complex multi-column tables switch to horizontal pan containers or progressive card stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `space-md` (16px) gutters and `margin` (16px) canvas boundaries.

### Layout Philosophy
Maintain strict horizontal baseline rhythm based on an 8pt increment (`0.5rem`). Information-heavy administrative views prioritize structural grouping over decorative separation, isolating workflow steps within clear visual boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth
The design relies on **Architectural Planar Depth** rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing an atmosphere of efficiency, clarity, and precision.

- **Foundational Canvas**: Backing layer utilizing `#F8FAFC` to create contrast against elevated operational modules.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` layered with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). No shadow is cast at rest to preserve minimalism.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Cards)**: Elevated through an ambient, multi-layered low-contrast shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.03)`. Border shifts subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Flyouts & Modals)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` with structural border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) reinforced by an executive ambient diffuse shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.12), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(20, 43, 69, 0.04)`.
- **Glass Accents**: Used exclusively on floating administrative command bars and sticky executive navigation: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses controlled, soft-radius geometry (`roundedness: 1`), conveying structured discipline and high-end executive software aesthetics:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Table Rows**: `0.25rem` (4px). Instills a disciplined, tailored, and professional feeling.
- **Cards, Panels, and Modal Containers**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Creates a cohesive, modern boundary without appearing playful or informal.
- **Status Badges & Chips**: Either `0.25rem` (4px) for architectural alignment or full pill (`rounded-full`) specifically for operational status indicators.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules**: Pure 1px solid lines using precise gray tone `#E2E8F0`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Navy (`#142B45`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius. Height: `40px` (Desktop) / `44px` (Touch). Hover state: `#254E70` with subtle translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#142B45`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border color `#142B45`.
- **Strategic / Premium Action**: Tertiary Gold (`#C5A46D`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text, exclusively reserved for high-value B2B conversion milestones or premium service tier requests.

### Data Tables (B2B Administrative Core)
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, text `#64748B`, uppercase `label-sm` tracking at `0.04em`, height `44px`, hairline border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Rows**: `#FFFFFF` background, height `52px`, hover state tint `#F8FAFC` with smooth transition (`150ms ease`).
- **Dividers**: Clean hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`). Monospaced numeric alignment for metrics, invoice IDs, and transaction statuses.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Success / Validated**: Pine Green background tint (`#E8F5F1`), border `1px solid #B8E4D7`, text `#16856A`.
- **In Progress / Pending**: Slate Blue tint (`#EDF3F8`), border `1px solid #C4D7E5`, text `#254E70`.
- **Strategic / Review**: Discreet Gold tint (`#FAF5EE`), border `1px solid #EEDBBE`, text `#96753C`.
- **Neutral / Draft**: Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#64748B`.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container**: Minimal height `42px`, crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` surface, font `body-md`.
- **Focus State**: Hairline ring `2px` with `#142B45` at `15%` opacity, border color shifting to `#142B45`. No aggressive neon glow.
- **Labels**: `label-md` in `#142B45` with optional micro helper text in `#64748B`.

### Cards & Service Panels
- White canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with uniform hairline border (`#E2E8F0`). Padding follows `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal hierarchy relies on structural sub-dividers rather than varying surface fills. Header, body, and action zones clearly delineated.
